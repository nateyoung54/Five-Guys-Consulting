What we do

Consulting for the systems that keep the counter moving.

We focus on the day-to-day workflows that determine whether a small business can keep up with demand: orders, inventory, reporting, forecasting, training, and system handoffs.

Featured project

BP Burger systems modernization

BP Burger was growing faster than its paper-based workflow could handle. Orders were written by hand, inventory was tracked manually, sales data was limited, and supplier orders were reactive instead of demand-driven.

Our charter proposes a structured SDLC implementation that connects POS, kitchen orders, inventory, supplier ordering, reporting, and forecasting into one practical operating model for Bud, Pearl, and their customers.

Fewer order errors

Electronic order capture and kitchen routing reduce the risk of missed or misread tickets.

Better purchasing

Inventory levels, delivery history, and usage data support smarter supplier orders.

Clearer decisions

Sales reports and trend data help owners understand what is selling and what to prepare for next.

How we work

A structured project path from interview to implementation.

  1. Plan

    Define scope, stakeholders, assumptions, and the rollout path.

  2. Analyze

    Interview users, observe current workflows, and document requirements.

  3. Design

    Create DFDs, ERDs, process maps, and the target system model.

  4. Build

    Configure the database, POS workflow, inventory logic, and reporting views.

  5. Test

    Validate orders, inventory updates, data accuracy, security, and usability.

  6. Launch

    Train users, deliver documentation, and support post-implementation adjustments.
